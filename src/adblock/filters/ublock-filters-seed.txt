! --- YouTube ad-serving/tracking network endpoints (youtube-adblock-gap
! investigation) -----------------------------------------------------------
! Added because EasyList/EasyPrivacy + the cosmetic rules above still let
! real YouTube video ads through: those two lists' generic ad/tracker
! domains (doubleclick.net, googlesyndication.com — both already in
! easylist-seed.txt) cover YouTube's THIRD-PARTY ad partners, but YouTube
! also drives ad selection/tracking through its OWN first-party endpoints,
! which a generic list intentionally does not touch (blocking a whole
! first-party domain is exactly what risks breaking the site). These are
! the specific, narrow, well-documented YouTube-owned paths used only for
! ad requests/impression-tracking, not video delivery itself:
!   - /pagead/            -> ad-serving requests (YouTube's own proxy to
!                            Google's ad system for in-player/masthead ads)
!   - /api/stats/ads      -> ad impression/quartile tracking pings
!   - /ptracking           -> ad click/progress tracking pings
! None of these paths carry actual video/audio data, so blocking them does
! not affect playback of the real video.
! Deliberately NOT included here: anything on googlevideo.com. That is the
! same CDN YouTube uses to serve the actual video/audio stream the player
! needs, including (for many ad formats, especially server-side-inserted
! ones) the ad segments themselves — there is no reliable, safe way to
! distinguish an ad chunk from a content chunk by URL/domain alone, and a
! wholesale ||googlevideo.com^ rule (or a guessed query-string pattern)
! would risk silently breaking ordinary video playback, which the brief
! explicitly rules out. Residual in-player ad UI from ads delivered this
! way is handled by the separate DOM-level ad-skip/hide layer in
! adblock/index.js (_applyCosmeticFiltering, which also injects
! YOUTUBE_AD_SKIP_SCRIPT), NOT by a network rule — that layer is a
! cosmetic supplement, not a network block, and is documented as such.
||youtube.com/pagead/^
||youtube.com/api/stats/ads^
||youtube.com/ptracking^
||s.youtube.com/api/stats/ads^
